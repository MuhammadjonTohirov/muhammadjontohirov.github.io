Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

TOX LLC ("we", "our", or "us") built PDF Booklet Maker as a commercial application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information Collection and Use

PDF Booklet Maker processes your PDF files and scanned documents on your device. We do not upload your PDF content to our servers, and we do not have access to the files you open or create with the app.

Advertising

PDF Booklet Maker uses Google AdMob to show ads in the free version of the app. Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device to provide, measure, and improve ads.

This information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interaction data, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and performance data. Depending on your region and privacy choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Consent and Privacy Choices

For users in regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, PDF Booklet Maker may show a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform.

When privacy options are available, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices inside the app.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the app may ask for permission to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. This permission is used for personalized advertising and ad measurement. You can allow or deny this permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Data Storage

Any files you process with the app remain on your device unless you choose to export, print, or share them using system features. We do not upload, share, or transfer your documents to any server or third party for booklet conversion.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob for advertising, ad measurement, and consent management.
  • Firebase for analytics, crash reporting, messaging, and diagnostics where enabled.

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob.

Children's Privacy

PDF Booklet Maker is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising and diagnostic services may process limited device or usage information as described in this policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
